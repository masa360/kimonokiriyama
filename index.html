About this experience

Wear a traditional Japanese kimono and take your own photos in a space overlooking Osaka Castle. Photos are taken indoors, so the weather never gets in the way — and on a fine day you can also step out into the garden, which looks onto the castle.

Please note

  • Please arrive 10 minutes before your start time.
  • Photos are taken with your own smartphone (self-portrait style).
  • Props are available for your photos: a traditional paper umbrella and a replica sword (not a real blade).
  • Our staff speak Japanese only. We use translation apps to assist you.
  • Children's kimonos are Shichi-Go-San sizes, so ages 3 to 7 are the guide.
  • Please contact us in advance if you need a larger size.
